Monthly report · October

Festive season lifted every store

Prepared by the operations team · 4 min read

Revenue grew 14% month on month, led by the new window displays. Two stores need extra floor staff before Diwali.

What went well

Footfall rose at every location after the festive displays went up, with the biggest gains at Wadala and Sion. Average order value climbed as shoppers bought complete outfits rather than single items.

What needs attention

Sion is still two floor staff short, and checkout queues peaked at 18 minutes on Saturday evenings. The billing printer outage at District 9 cost an estimated half day of sales.

Next month

Extend the display to District 9 by Friday, hire two seasonal staff for Sion, and share the live dashboard with regional leads.
