Live store dashboards

Give every store manager one page with today's numbers, instead of a weekly spreadsheet.

Problem

  • Numbers arrive a week late, by email
  • Every store builds its own spreadsheet
  • Nobody knows which version is current

Proposal

  • One shared dashboard per store
  • Connected to the sales sheet, refreshed hourly
  • Comments for questions, right on the numbers

Plan

Week 1Build the dashboard for Wadala and test with the store manager
Week 2Connect the live sales sheet and roll out to four stores
Week 3Review feedback, then share with regional leads
