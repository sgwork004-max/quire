# Quire desktop app shell

Scope: the whole desktop app chrome (library, viewer, editors, dialogs), app icon, logo and wordmark. Mode: Operate.
Audience and job: AI-native operators keeping AI-made HTML findable, viewing it, making small edits and sharing it. The user's HTML always renders untouched; the identity lives in the chrome around it.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A print studio's discipline in a calm Mac app: inks carry meaning, never decoration. Refuses the grey-and-purple SaaS shell and the cream-serif-terracotta default. (User kept the feel of the rolled Duplicator Studio but chose their own palette, layout and icon.)

OWN-WORLD: Cool grey stock with faint grain on the chrome only, near-black ink type, two inks with fixed meaning: federal blue #3d5588 is you (selection, your actions, focus, stars), sunflower #ffb511 is other people (presence, comments and pins, shared, notifications, conflicts from outside). Overprint green where both apply. Gone/resolved in grey ink. Dark mode: charcoal stock, lighter blue, same sunflower. Display face Bricolage Grotesque for wordmark and page titles only; UI on the system stack.

STORY: At a glance the user tells what is private and theirs (blue) from what other people are in (sunflower), and trusts nothing leaves without an explicit Share.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Today's layout (user's Layout A): sidebar with wordmark beside the traffic lights, tabs on top, thumbnail grid library; Share is the one solid blue button; collaborators' avatars and comment pins in sunflower.

FORM: Duplicator Studio feel, candidate 7 of my grounded list, seed key 731a5c20; palette, layout and icon pinned by the user (palette 2, layout A, icon 3: misregistered grey q over a sunflower pass on a federal-blue tile).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
